Earth — 2050
Embryosis Corp controls more than half of the Eastern Hemisphere.Its state uses AI and mass surveillance to maintain order as humanity struggles to adapt to its greatest creation. In 2040, the head of Embryosis was assassinated by a member of the Free Radicals 

A group of rebels and activists who believed humanity needed a more careful, symbiotic relationship with AI.

The Free Radicals were blamed for the killing.What followed was a brutal five-year war between Embryosis Corp and the Free Radicals. 

Among those drafted was Bradlee Bishop, who served as a combat medic for Embryosis. The war left him with deep psychological scars and memories he could never escape.

It is now 2050, and Bradlee works as a journalist for the Chicago Times. Haunted by his past and suspicious of Embryosis, he has secretly boarded the maiden voyage of the world's largest nuclear submarine.

Its name is Leviathan.

A new chapter in science and human history is about to begin. Within Leviathan lies the world's largest AI data centre, But Bradlee isn't there to witness history. He's there to find Hugo Denvers And to get the interview he's been chasing for years.
